Hello — I'm Vinaya Kalidindi, about to complete my education in Bachelor of Applied Biotechnology
(Honours) program at Conestoga College. I hold an Advanced Diploma in Biotechnology - Advanced from Algonquin College as well

My professional interests include microbiology, molecular biology, quality control/ regulatory affairs, antimicrobial resistance, 
and the use of computational methods to support biological research. I am particularly interested in opportunities that combine 
practical laboratory work with data analysis and scientific communication.

Bioinformatics is relevant to my career goals because it allows me to retrieve, compare, visualize, and interpret biological 
data while connecting computational findings with experimental results. Through BIOT74000, I have developed
experience with biological databases, sequence-analysis tools, file formats, data visualization, and introductory computational methods.

This portfolio documents the bioinformatics tools, methods, and skills I have
developed, and how I have applied them.
